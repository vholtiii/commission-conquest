on kenney.nl. The sedan faces +Z in the file and is about 2.55 long.

  car-sedan.glb
  Textures/colormap.png   referenced by the GLB as Textures/colormap.png

Cinematic cars load these. The open-map street fleet stays procedural.
Other Kenney kits can be dropped in this folder the same way.
